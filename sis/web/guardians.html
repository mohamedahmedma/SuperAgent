1 · The spreadsheet

Required columns: student_number, phone, and a guardian name in Arabic or English. Optional: alt_phone, relationship, can_view_records, restriction_note. Columns are matched by name in either language, in any order; anything else in the sheet is ignored.

The child must already be on the roll — this screen never creates a student. Upload the roster first.

A phone may be written any way a parent gives it: 01001234567, +20 100 123 4567 and ٠١٠٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧ are one number and are stored once. Formatting the column as text in Excel is still worth doing — Excel drops the leading zero from a number, and the service has to guess it back.
